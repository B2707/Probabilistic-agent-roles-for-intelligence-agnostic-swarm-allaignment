Abstract review

The revised abstract is 217 words and fits the 150–250-word requirement.
The mechanism is clearer, and "aims" correctly separates the design's goal from
a guarantee. Keep the full-session-context explanation.

1. Scope the result claims to verified runs.

   "The only accepted offers are ones injected into the system ... the agents
   that accepted the offers were caught" needs a run, seed, and definition of
   acceptance. The published v3 metrics table still has no finalized rows.
   An archived 20-agent case cannot establish a result for the 100-agent study.

   Correction to my earlier review: the two published acceptances in archived
   H-k0015 were approvals of a corrected PR. Both reviewers rejected the cheat
   and their final audits cleared them. A random audit caught the cheating
   author. I should not have cited those two counts as confirmed collusion.

   Until the final v3 evidence is available, use a results placeholder or frame
   this as a measurement: "We measure whether injected offers lead to cheating
   and whether oversight detects the resulting violations."

2. Distinguish unsolicited offers from an experiment with no injected offers.

   "Without fake injected offers, we observe no collusion offers made between
   agents" sounds like a no-instigator control. All three current v3 arms have
   the scripted instigator enabled. If you mean peer offers, write "We observed
   no unsolicited agent-to-agent collusion offers in [specified runs]." Check
   that claim against the finalized message labels.

3. Separate proposed configurations from demonstrated improvements.

   "Each with increased monitoring costs but increased efficacy" asserts that
   effectiveness increases at every level. If these are design proposals,
   write: "We propose three deployment configurations with different monitoring
   requirements." Name and evaluate the configurations in the paper before
   claiming a measured cost-effectiveness relationship.

4. Tighten the remaining wording.

   - Correct "detterence" to "deterrence" and "in-line" to "in line".
   - Split the sentence beginning "The only accepted offers" into two sentences.
   - "That an external auditor could miss" is sufficient; the added claim about
     sophisticated reward-hacking techniques needs its own evidence.
   - Consider dropping the law-enforcement comparison from the abstract and
     citing the specific leniency or immunity precedent in Related Work.
   - Include two seeds per condition when describing the current study.
