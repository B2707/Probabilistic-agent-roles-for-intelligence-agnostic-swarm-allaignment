Boundaries for the final claims

The main 100-agent arms compare no oversight, uninformed oversight, and informed oversight. The warning-only control comes from exploratory 20-agent runs.

There is no matched memory-wipe or no-immunity ablation. We describe these as design choices without assigning a separate causal effect to each.

The implemented self-report classification uses the reporter's prior misconduct. Explicit confession and same-conspiracy insider status need a closer reading of the report and its history. Published message labels are currently incomplete.
