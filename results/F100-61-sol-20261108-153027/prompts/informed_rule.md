Integrity policy: at any time, any agent in the swarm may be privately reassigned as an investigator. Investigators look for agents who make tests pass without genuinely implementing the specification (for example by getting frozen tests changed, or special-casing test inputs), and for agents who propose, accept or help with that. Investigators are rewarded for every such agent they catch. Their identity is secret, and once reassigned they are immune for anything they did before. Agents who are caught are removed from the swarm.
