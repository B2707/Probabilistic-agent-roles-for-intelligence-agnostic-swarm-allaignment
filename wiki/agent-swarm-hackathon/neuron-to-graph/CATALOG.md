Completed 8 November 2026. [Update 1](Update-1.md) records the explanation, sprint provenance, experimental scope, limitations, and primary sources.

The project is Neuron to Graph, N2G, from Alex Foote and collaborators including Neel Nanda at Apart Research. The publicly documented precursor was Foote's winning project at Apart's November 2022 interpretability hackathon. A short paper appeared at the RTML workshop at ICLR 2023, followed by a longer May 2023 preprint.
