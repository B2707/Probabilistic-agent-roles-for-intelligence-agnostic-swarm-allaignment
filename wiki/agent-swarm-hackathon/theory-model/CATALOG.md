The terminal payoff is 1 exactly when the original agent survives to evaluation
with a qualifying successful task outcome or at least one eligible confirmed
catch of another agent; it is 0 otherwise. Reporting only oneself and conversion
alone earn no catch reward. Removal overrides earlier success. Additional catches
do not add utility, and there is no separate fine or per-action utility cost.
Workers may anticipate future investigator success. These choices are settled;
do not ask for them again.

Do not ask again for P/K definitions, offer rules, or role rewards; these were
verified in the source. Conditional success, evidence, and response probabilities
remain model inputs. Known P,K alone do not determine a unique best action or
prove deterrence. Current live scoring has not been changed to this binary rule.


The new sender example conditions on a worker author and makes that known to the
receiver. It uses a two-person task abstraction and the actual one-round message
delay. At b=1, P=1.5%, K=1%, receiver acceptance is 67.76% versus 21.95%
refusal, and worker initiation gives 59.78% versus a zero no-offer baseline.
Task-success paths contribute 58.49 points and catch-without-task paths 1.28.
At b=0 the receiver refuses and initiation creates no catch, so its payoff is
zero. Acceptance begins above b=8.67%. These values differ from the original
three-person clean-worker chart because the horizon, role conditioning, and
timing differ. They are a restricted best-response example, not a full
signalling equilibrium or a replacement for the conspiracy-size model.
An offer alone identifies an investigator only under identified positive
investigator offer rates and zero worker offer rates. Both rates zero leaves
Bayes undefined. Investigator authors of new prohibited offers face nonimmune
misconduct and reporter audits; do not assume cost-free investigator baiting.
