Measurement and source

There are no explicit audit-start timestamps. Each round estimate starts at its last recorded turn event and ends when the batch verdicts appear. Nearby state-saving and verdict processing are included. Auditors run concurrently, so their individual call times are not added together.

Two primary Sol seed-1 runs have interruptions without a stop marker. Their unlogged gaps are excluded because the logs cannot separate active work from downtime. These rows carry a warning. Three original interrupted Luna seed-1 extras are kept in the evidence file and excluded from this chart.
